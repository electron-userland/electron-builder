feat(runtime)!: an `HttpExecutor` without a cross-origin header policy of its own, such as builder-util's `httpExecutor`, sends a redirect to another origin only the headers that carry no credential (`Accept`, `Accept-Encoding`, `Accept-Language`, `Cache-Control`, `Content-Encoding`, `Content-Language`, `Content-Length`, `Content-Type`, `If-Match`, `If-Modified-Since`, `If-None-Match`, `If-Range`, `If-Unmodified-Since`, `Pragma`, `Range`, `User-Agent`) and leaves out the well-known credential headers (`Authorization`, `Proxy-Authorization`, `Cookie`, `PRIVATE-TOKEN`, `X-Api-Key`, `X-Auth-Token`, `X-Access-Token`, `X-GitLab-Token`, `X-CSRF-Token`). Any other header, a custom one such as `X-Api-Token` too, fails the request with `ERR_CROSS_ORIGIN_HEADERS_NOT_KEPT`, whose message names those headers and both origins (never a value): pass the executor a `CrossOriginHeaderPolicy` that keeps or leaves out the header (a custom publisher constructs its own `NodeHttpExecutor` with one), or stop sending it. electron-updater keeps its own policy (`autoUpdater.crossOriginHeaders`). https://www.electron.build/docs/migration/v27-developer-changes#builder-util-runtime-default-cross-origin-headers
